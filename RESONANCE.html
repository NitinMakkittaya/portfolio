Case File #010: Resonance — Emotion-Adaptive Music Player

III. INVESTIGATOR'S SUMMARY

A subject's micro-expressions betray their internal state long before they speak. The objective of Operation Resonance was to build a biometric emotion-adaptive audio unit capable of reading a subject's face in real time and dynamically altering both the acoustic environment and the visual interface to match their psychological profile. By splicing an OpenCV and DeepFace optical wiretap directly into Spotify's playback engine, the system eliminates manual playlist curation entirely.

IV. EVIDENCE LOG (Tech Stack)

  • The Optical Wiretap: OpenCV (Real-time webcam frame capture, bounding-box isolation, and facial landmark tracking)
  • The Polygraph Brain: DeepFace Neural Framework (Facial expression classification across Happy, Sad, Angry, Neutral, Surprise, and Fear states)
  • The Acoustic Dispatch: Spotify Integration Engine (Curates and embeds mood-matched audio streams and recommendations)
  • The Chameleon Shell: Dynamic Emotion-Driven UI (Morphs ambient colour palettes, lighting, and interface themes in sync with detected affect)
  • Repository Archive: Emotion_player

V. MODUS OPERANDI (How It Works)

Resonance operates as a closed-loop biometric feedback pipeline:

  • Biometric Frame Intercept: OpenCV captures live video frames from the subject's optical sensor, isolating the facial region and normalizing lighting variations for neural inference.
  • DeepFace Affect Classification: The cropped facial tensor is passed through DeepFace's convolutional neural network to extract a probability distribution across core human emotions, locking onto the dominant affective state.
  • Sonic & Visual Synchronization: Once an emotional shift is verified, the system triggers a dual payload—dispatching emotion-calibrated music recommendations through the Spotify player while smoothly morphing the UI's entire colour scheme to reflect the subject's mood.

VI. THE VERDICT

Resonance bridges computer vision and human-computer interaction into a seamless ambient experience. By coupling low-latency OpenCV/DeepFace emotion inference with Spotify playback and reactive CSS theming, the application responds instantaneously to the user's unspoken emotional state.

VII. CASE ASSETS

  • Real-Time Facial Telemetry: Continuous, non-intrusive affect recognition powered by OpenCV and DeepFace.
  • Emotion-Mapped Spotify Pipeline: Automatically maps psychological telemetry to curated acoustic genres and embedded Spotify tracks.
  • Reactive Atmospheric UI: Dynamic state-driven interface styling that shifts hues, contrast, and ambience alongside the listener's mood.
